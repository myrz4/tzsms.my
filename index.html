Why centres switch

Manual attendance, scattered parent updates, and billing mistakes create avoidable daily pressure.

When attendance lives in one book, parent communication lives in chat threads, and billing lives in separate files, routine centre work becomes slower to check and easier to get wrong.

  • 1Drop-off slows downManual check-ins take time, especially when staff need to confirm the same information more than once.
  • 2Pickup confirmation gets fragmentedWithout one workflow, staff and parents rely on disconnected messages and manual follow-up.
  • 3Billing and reporting need reworkDouble entry increases the risk of missed invoices, inconsistent records, and more admin overhead.

FAQ

Practical questions from centres and families.

Does the system support NFC and QR attendance?
Yes. Smart Childcare System supports NFC attendance scanning together with QR attendance and pickup workflows, so centres can use the process that fits each part of the day.
Can parents view attendance information?
Yes. The parent app gives families access to attendance information, along with notifications and chat support from the same connected system.
Is billing included in the system?
Yes. Billing and invoice support are part of the platform, alongside student records, reporting, and attendance management.
What is the difference between the desktop app and the mobile apps?
The admin desktop package is for centre management, reporting, records, and billing control. The teacher app is for classroom-side workflow and QR tools. The parent app focuses on attendance visibility, communication, and family access.
Is the system suitable for taskas and preschools?
Yes. The system is positioned for taskas and preschools that want one connected platform for attendance, records, billing, reporting, and parent communication.
